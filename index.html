Message from the MLA

Together, for a brighter Jhanjharpur.

Our constituency stretches across the historic plains of Mithila, blessed with the Kamala and Kosi rivers and a vibrant cultural heritage. Over the last term, we have focused on three pillars — infrastructure, education and livelihood — and this portal is built to keep every citizen informed of the progress on each promise.

From the weekly Janta Darbar to a 24×7 grievance helpdesk, we are committed to a government that listens, responds and delivers.
